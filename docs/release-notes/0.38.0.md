The chat's composer is now a band across the bottom of the chat, as in Zed,
and its controls stay on one line however narrow the chat gets.

**The composer is a band**

- It runs the full width of the chat under the conversation, with a thin
  rule above it, and its text lines up with the conversation's. With the
  session panel docked beside the chat, it stops at the panel.
- Attach (now a +), the session panel and ⋯ sit on the left. The agent's
  controls and send sit on the right.
- The permission mode is an icon: a hand for the default mode, a pen for
  accepting edits, a clipboard for plan, sparkles for auto, and a red
  struck-through shield for Bypass. The connection's dot sits on its
  corner. In a wide chat the mode's name stays beside it, and the tooltip
  always has it.

**One line at every width**

The controls no longer wrap onto a second line. As the chat narrows they
make room in steps, and what leaves the line becomes a row of ⋯:

- First Fast, Thinking and the session panel toggle move into ⋯, and the
  mode shows only its icon.
- Then the background-task count and the context ring.
- Then the model, or the agent's name, and attach, and the chips that stay
  lose their arrows. Down to the narrowest pane Sirio allows, the mode, the
  effort and send stay on the line. There *Default* shows as empty bars and
  Ultracode as full ones, and the tooltip names the level.

A picker you open from ⋯ opens there, inside the chat. ⋯ marks the first
row you can choose, and the mark stays on its row when the rows change
under it, for example when a background task ends. Nothing covers send.
