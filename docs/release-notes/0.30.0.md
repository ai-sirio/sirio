The chat's composer is quieter and its thinking indicator is the orb again.
The Neutral theme is darker, and the browser has a mark of its own.

**The chat**

- **No key hints under the composer.** The line under the card that said what
  Enter and Shift+Enter do is gone. The keys have not changed: Enter sends, or
  queues while the agent is working, and Shift+Enter starts a new line.
- **The orb is back.** While the agent works, the Thinking line and the
  header of a thought being written show the animated orb again, in place of
  0.29.0's sparkles.
- **The Thinking line** now starts at the composer card's left edge and sits a
  little higher, clear of the card's top border.
- **The stop button can be seen.** While the agent works, the button that
  stops it is drawn solid, like the send button. It had almost the same colour
  as the composer, so all that showed was a thin outlined square.

**A darker Neutral**

The dark Neutral theme read as too grey, so every surface is a step darker:
central panes `#0E0E0E` (was `#141414`), sidebars `#131313` (`#191919`), the
chat `#1A1A1A` (`#232323`), the composer `#242424` (`#313131`), and the hover
`#292929` (`#363636`). Light Neutral and the other base colours are
unchanged.

**Icons**

- The browser has its own mark, a monitor, in its tab, on the launcher tile
  and in the New Browser menu row. It used to share the globe with other
  things.
- The icons on the launcher tiles of an empty pane are larger.
- The sidebar's Search worktrees field has a magnifying glass icon, in place
  of a text character.
