Chat replies in Sirio now show more about themselves: they appear at a steady
pace while they stream, a failed turn can be retried in place, and a usage
limit counts down to its reset.

**Replies that stream in**

- A reply appears at a steady pace as it arrives, with a caret after the last
  word. Stopping, or the end of the turn, shows the rest at once. With the
  system's reduced motion turned on, the reply is not paced.
- Long replies cost much less to draw while they stream. On a 40 KiB reply,
  each new chunk takes about a sixth of the time it did before. A reply
  written without blank lines still costs what it did, and is drawn without
  pacing once it grows long.

**A failed turn says so, and Retry sends it again**

- When a turn fails, for example because Claude is overloaded, the chat keeps
  what had arrived and adds an error row with **Retry**. Before, Claude's
  error was appended to the reply's text.
- Retry sends your message again, in the same conversation when the agent
  still has it. Before, it only restarted the agent. Only the newest failed
  turn keeps its Retry.
- Messages you queued behind a failed turn wait under "Waiting: press Retry or
  send a message." until you retry or send something.

**Usage limits**

When Claude's usage limit is reached, the chat shows "Usage limit reached"
with a countdown to the reset, then "Try again" or "Usage limit lifted".

**Days and thoughts**

- Days in the chat are marked by a separator in plain case: "Today",
  "Yesterday", "Friday, November 6". They used to be uppercase headings such
  as "FRI 6 NOV".
- Claude's thinking shows its duration ("Thought for N seconds") from the
  moment its thinking block ends, not when the next part of the reply starts.
