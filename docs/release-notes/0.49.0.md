Chat messages in Sirio now have actions: you can copy any message, quote a
reply into your next prompt, and continue a reply that was cut short.

**A row of actions on each message**

- Each message has a row of actions under it. The last reply always shows it;
  other messages show it when the pointer is over them.
- **Copy** puts the message's Markdown text on the clipboard. Your own
  messages have Copy too.
- Your messages are a little taller, to leave room for their row.

**Quote**

- **Quote** on a message puts it above the composer, and your next message
  starts with it as a quote. To quote only part of a reply, select it and pick
  **Quote selection** from the chat's right-click menu. The ✕ on the quote
  removes it.
- A command such as `/compact` is sent without the quote, which stays for your
  next message.
- The message you sent shows its quote as a card, also when you reopen the
  chat.

**Continue**

- When a reply stops at the token limit, at the turn limit or because you
  pressed Stop, the last reply offers **Continue**. It sends "Continue" as
  your next message, in the same conversation.
- Continue is not offered while a turn runs, in a chat reopened from history,
  or after Codex, OpenCode, Pi or Oh-My-Pi restarted, since the agent no
  longer has the conversation.

**Stopping Claude**

- A Claude turn you stop now ends as "cancelled". It used to read "ended",
  and Claude's closing text was added to the reply.
- A Claude turn that reaches its turn limit now says "turn-request limit".
