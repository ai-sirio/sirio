This release is small and all repair-and-polish: the Files panel learns to
keep a checkout's clutter out of sight, and two things that were painted
wrong stop being painted wrong.

**Hidden entries are a switch, not a rule**

A checkout's dotfiles — `.env`, `.github`, `.cargo` — sat in the Files tree
beside the files you were actually reading. They are hidden now by default,
and the panel's bottom-right corner carries the two icons that change that:
an eye and a crossed-out eye, whichever is active filled in. The choice
belongs to the session rather than to one worktree, so releasing the dotfiles
in one checkout releases them everywhere until the app is quit; a fresh
launch starts hidden again.

**The effort slider stays inside its card**

The 14px knob at the end of the effort slider was centred on the track's
ends, so half of it hung past each one and clipped into the popover's 4px
padding. The scale is inset by 8px and the knob now clears the border.

**A tooltip stops showing the window through it**

The commit tooltip in History and the status bar's tooltips were painted on a
raised surface, which the app's translucency fades — so the rows behind them
read straight through the card. Both now paint on the same opaque surface the
context menus already use.
