Sirio now asks for a project's name and icon when you add it, keeps your
saved prompts in the right panel, and lets the chats and terminals fold into
a narrow rail. In the chat, ↑ brings back what you sent and Shift+Tab moves
to the next permission mode.

**A name and an icon for a new project**

After you add a project, with Open folder, Clone, Create, the empty centre
or the prompt for a folder that is not a git repository, a *Project added*
dialog asks for the name the sidebar shows and for the project's icon.

- The folder on disk keeps its own name.
- Esc or Skip keeps the defaults.
- Projects added over the control socket never open it.
  `sirioctl project add` takes `--display-name` instead.

**Saved prompts**

The right panel has a *Prompts* section for the prompts you send again and
again.

- One click on a prompt's copy button puts its text on the clipboard.
- The pencil, or a click on the card, opens the prompt in a centre tab with
  a title field. It saves itself 400 ms after you stop typing, and also on
  Ctrl/Cmd+S, on a worktree switch, when you close its tab and when you quit.
- *New prompt* adds one, and right-click › Delete removes one after asking.
- Each prompt is a Markdown file, holding exactly the text Copy copies, in
  `~/.local/share/Sirio/prompts` on Linux,
  `~/Library/Application Support/Sirio/prompts` on macOS and
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Sirio\prompts` on Windows.
- When the last file in a tab closes and a terminal is split beside it,
  the tab becomes a terminal tab, and comes back as one after a restart.

**Fold the chats and terminals into a rail**

The half that holds the chats and terminals has a new button to the right of
its +. It folds the half into a 40 px rail, and the editor, Changes and the
browser take the width.

- The rail has one icon per open chat or terminal, with its status dot, a
  button on top that unfolds the half again, and a + at its foot.
- A click on an icon shows that chat or terminal in a card over the editor,
  as wide as the half was. A click outside the card closes it. Esc does not:
  it still goes to the chat's Cancel or to the terminal.
- Folding changes only what is drawn. Chats keep answering and terminals
  keep running, and what they wrote while folded is there when you look.
- Sirio remembers, per worktree, whether the half is folded.

**Recall a prompt, cycle the mode**

- In an empty composer, ↑ brings back the prompts sent in this chat, newest
  first, with their `@` mentions, and ↓ walks forward again. The last 50
  are kept with the chat and survive a restart. With text in the box, or
  the caret off its first or last line, the arrows move the caret as before.
- Shift+Tab in the composer moves to the agent's next permission mode. It
  never lands on Bypass, which still asks first when you pick it.

**Fixes**

- Ctrl+Shift+P no longer opens the command palette over a close that is
  waiting for an answer, the End All confirmation or the Set Title prompt.
- A closed tab's pill no longer stays on its project's row in the sidebar.
- `sirioctl project list` shows a project's new name right after it is
  changed in Project Settings.
- The name field in Project Settings no longer shrinks to a few pixels in a
  short tab.
