A Claude chat can now show its agents' work as a timeline, the session
panel opens with its cards folded, and dialogs and menus stay solid and
readable over a translucent window.

**A trace of the agents' work**

The session panel of a Claude Code chat has a new card, *Trace*, between
Connectors and Change request. It answers how the current turn is going:
which agents are running, which one is waiting for you, what failed, how
long each step took and what the turn cost.

- Folded, the card is one line: *Live*, *Waiting for you*, *Done*,
  *Interrupted* or *Failed*, with the time and, when it is known, the cost.
- Unfolded, it shows one lane per agent on a shared time axis, subagents
  included. A failed call is red and a wait for your answer is amber.
  Clicking a lane or a wait scrolls the transcript to that row.
- A tab in the right half shows the whole turn as a tree beside its
  timeline. You can step back through earlier turns or follow the latest
  one, and move through it with the arrow keys; Enter shows the row in the
  chat. There is one per chat, and it closes with its chat.
- Nothing moves once the turn has ended, or while the card or tab is out of
  sight.

The trace is for chats that talk to Claude Code directly. Other agents do
not report their subagents in a way Sirio can draw, so they have no Trace
card.

**A quieter session panel**

The panel's cards now start folded, except Environment, so you open the ones
you need. The Change request card no longer asks `gh` or `glab` anything to
decide whether to appear, and a forge you are not signed in to reads *Not
signed in to <host>* instead of hiding the card.

**Fixes**

- With translucency on, dialogs, menus, popovers and tooltips were
  see-through, and the text of some dialogs (*Bypass permissions?*, *Discard*,
  the review, hand-off and merge confirmations) was black on a dark card. They
  are now opaque, with text in the theme's colour, and the labels on coloured
  buttons and badges keep their full strength.
- When a subagent asked for a permission, the chat no longer leaves a tool row
  stuck as running in the transcript.
